Shahir Abdullah is a Master's student in Computer Science and Engineering at United International University (UIU), Bangladesh, pursuing a thesis-based degree with research interests in Human-Computer Interaction, AI agents, adaptive UI systems, distributed systems, and computational optimization.

He completed his B.Sc. in Computer Science and Engineering from the Military Institute of Science and Technology (MIST), Dhaka, in 2021. During his undergraduate studies, he developed an assistive Bangla book-reader system for visually impaired users, combining Android, Arduino-based hardware, computer vision, machine learning, and text-to-speech technologies.

Currently, Shahir works as a Software Engineer at Ajentica, where he has nearly four years of professional experience developing .NET/C# and Angular-based applications. His work focuses on full-stack software engineering, backend/API development, distributed application architecture, and AI-agent integration. He is currently involved in LLM-based desktop UI automation, exploring structured UI representations, workflow-aware UI grounding, graph-based UI optimization, and adaptive automation for changing software interfaces.

His research interests lie at the intersection of AI agents, graph-based optimization, Resource aware computing, HCI, UI automation, digital accessibility, adaptive systems, distributed systems. He is particularly interested in building intelligent systems that can reason about complex environments, adapt to changing conditions, and interact reliably with humans and software systems.
